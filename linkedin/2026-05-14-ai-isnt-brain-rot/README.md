AI isn’t “brain rot.”

What most people call a chatbot is actually a retrieval and synthesis engine.

I think in terms of AKUs:
Atomic Knowledge Units.

Small fragments of:
- observations
- experiences
- research
- conversations
- location/context
- workflows
- memories
- decisions

The real skill isn’t memorizing everything anymore.

It’s building systems that:
- accumulate AKUs
- structure them
- retrieve them efficiently
- recombine them into context-specific intelligence

That changes what expertise means.

A decade of experience alone no longer guarantees leverage.

The advantage now goes to the person who can:
- ask better questions
- orchestrate better retrieval
- synthesize faster
- adapt in real time

High-level consulting has always been partially a retrieval problem.
AI just exposed it.

Most people are still “talking to AI.”

I’m more interested in building cognitive infrastructure around people, families, and teams:

- personal memory systems
- behavioral patterning
- context-aware retrieval
- data sovereignty
- human-in-the-loop intelligence

If data brokers are going to build predictive models of us, we should probably build our own first.

At this point I’ve basically become the family DD:
Designated Data Scientist.. and im awake at the wheel.

That’s part of what I’ve been building:
FamilyOS.

The future isn’t just personalized AI.
It’s self-owned intelligence infrastructure.

More soon.
